See a standard curb ramp at your corner

A true-scale City of Seattle Type 422A curb ramp you can place on the street with your phone's camera, to check roughly what would fit: is there room, and what's in the way?

3D model

AR isn't available on this device. You can still rotate and zoom the 3D model.
Stay on the sidewalk. Place the ramp by pointing your phone at the street from the curb. Never step into the street or traffic to use this.

How to use it

  1. Open AR. Tap "View at true scale in AR" on a phone. Android uses Chrome's AR, iPhone and iPad use AR Quick Look.
  2. Let it find the street. Slowly move the phone so it can detect the pavement.
  3. Place it at the curb. The model sits on the street surface. Aim at the street right in front of the curb, where a ramp would meet the street, and tap to place.
  4. Line it up. Turn it with two fingers until the yellow panel faces the street and the model's curb lines up with the real one. The size is locked to true scale.
  5. Look around. Poles, hydrants and signs need 1 ft of clearance from the ramp and landing; drains and utility covers can't sit in them. Take a screenshot to attach to your request.

What you're looking at

A perpendicular ramp built to Seattle's Standard Plan 422a and 422k (2026 edition), for Seattle's standard 6 inch curb.

  • 5 ft wideramp (4 ft is the minimum)
  • 6 ft longrising 6 in at 8.3%, the steepest allowed
  • 5 ft wingson each side along the curb, at 10%
  • 4 ft landinglevel, at the top, as wide as the ramp
  • 2 ft yellow paneltruncated domes, about 2⅓ in apart, across the full width
  • 4 × 4 ft clear areadashed on the street, which must fall inside the crosswalk

Limits

  • This is a rough visual aid, not a design. SDOT engineers pick the ramp type and fit it to each corner.
  • Phone AR is usually accurate to within a few percent, but it can drift, and it assumes flat ground. Real streets and sidewalks slope, so the model may float or sink a little.
  • Corners where the ramp crosses a grass planting strip are often built with side curbs instead of wings, and corners usually get two ramps, one per crossing.
