Report a corner where you are

At an intersection anywhere in Seattle? Check its curb ramps, one corner at a time. Reports made on the spot correct the city's records on the hotspot map, and carry over to a street page if one is ever made for that street.

Dots are intersections: black, no curb ramp on record; green, ramps on record; gray, no record either way. An orange ring means a neighbor has reported it.

Your reports

Saved in this browser as you tick. Send them when you're done, from here, so they reach the map for everyone.

What happens to a report

  • Send opens a GitHub issue with your report filled in (you'll need a free GitHub account). It's public, so leave out your name, phone and email. Photos help: one per corner, taken from the sidewalk.
  • A maintainer checks the report and merges it. The next daily refresh puts it on the hotspot map, and on a street page if the intersection is on one.
  • On site or not. With your location on, a report records how far your phone was from the intersection, and how precise it was. Only those two numbers are saved, never your position. A report made within 300 ft overrides the city's records; one made from elsewhere is shown, but doesn't change the counts.
  • Corners are by compass. NE is the corner northeast of the middle of the intersection, and "cross west" means the crosswalk you'd take walking west from it. Where streets run at an angle, pick the closest.
  • Only Seattle for now: the intersections come from SDOT's inventory.
